Refund policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version. All payments for Bhagawan AI are non-refundable. That includes the ₹1 trial and every monthly renewal. You can cancel at any time to stop future renewals.

1. What you pay

Bhagawan AI costs ₹1 for a 1-day trial, then ₹199 per month, renewed automatically until you cancel. Prices are in Indian rupees and include applicable taxes. Payments are processed by Razorpay.

2. No refunds

Because Bhagawan AI is a digital service you can use as soon as you pay, we do not refund any payment once it has been made. This applies to:

  • the ₹1 trial payment;
  • every monthly renewal, including one charged because you forgot to cancel;
  • a subscription you did not use, or used only a little;
  • a change of mind about the app.

Please try the ₹1 trial and read these terms before you continue past it.

3. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from Profile inside the app. If you cancel before the next renewal date you will not be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Cancelling does not refund the current period.

4. Duplicate or wrongly charged payments

If you were charged twice for the same period, charged an amount different from the price shown, or charged after you had already cancelled, write to us at hello@astravanta.com within 7 days with the phone number on your account and the Razorpay payment ID. We will check it, and where the charge was our error we will reverse the extra amount to your original payment method. Reversals normally reach your account within 5 to 10 working days of approval.

5. The law

Nothing in this policy takes away a right you have under Indian law that cannot be waived by agreement.
