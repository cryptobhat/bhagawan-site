Questions people ask first

Am I really talking to God?

No. Bhagawan AI is an artificial intelligence that replies in the manner and spirit of each deity. It is meant as comfort and a prompt to reflect, not as a divine voice, and it is not a substitute for a priest or your own faith practice.

Which languages does it speak?

It is built for Hinglish, the way most of us text, and also understands Hindi and English. Write however feels natural.

How much does it cost?

₹1 for a 1-day trial, then ₹199 a month. Prices include applicable taxes.

Can I get a refund?

No. All payments, including the ₹1 trial and renewals, are non-refundable. You can cancel any time to stop the next renewal. The refund policy has the details.

How do I cancel?

Cancel from Profile inside the app, before the next renewal date. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Is my phone number safe?

We use it to sign you in with a one-time code and to link your subscription. We do not sell it. The privacy policy lists exactly who processes what.

Can family see my chats on a shared phone?

Use the private screen to hide your chats behind a tap, so they stay between you and your Bhagwan.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Bhagawan AI is on Android first.

How do I delete my data?

Use Delete account in the app, or email us from the address on your account. We will remove your profile and chat data. The privacy policy explains what we keep and for how long.
