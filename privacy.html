Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what Bhagawan AI (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our Android app and website, why, and the choices you have. Bhagawan AI is operated by Nagaraj Bhat, an individual based in India.

The short version. We collect what we need to sign you in, to reply to your messages, and to take payment. We do not sell your data and we do not use your chats for advertising. Your messages are sent to AI providers only to generate replies.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy
Mobile number and a sign-in tokenTo create and secure your account and send a one-time code
Your name (and age, if you give it)To address you and to make sure you are old enough
Your messages and the repliesTo run the chat, show your history, and let the AI remember what you told it
Subscription and payment records (plan, dates, Razorpay order and payment IDs)To give you access and to reconcile payments
Device and app details (push token, Android version, app version, IP address, crash reports)To send your daily message, prevent abuse, and fix bugs
Usage events (screens opened, buttons tapped)To understand which features are used and to improve the app

We do not collect your card or UPI details (Razorpay handles those), your precise location, your contacts, your photos, your microphone or your camera.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, generate replies, store your history and your daily message settings.
  • To take payment and manage your subscription.
  • To send notifications, only after you allow them.
  • To keep the service safe and to fix problems.
  • To comply with the law and respond to lawful requests.

3. Who processes it

We send specific data to specific providers so the app works. We do not give them wider access.

4. AI-generated replies

Replies come from a language model and can be inaccurate. They are not the words of any deity or religious authority, and they are not professional advice. Please do not share information you would not want processed by the providers listed above.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and chat history while your account is open. When you delete your account we remove your profile and chat data from our systems within 30 days. We may keep payment records for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting.

6. Your choices

  • Delete your account from inside the app, or email us from the address on your account.
  • Turn off notifications in your phone's settings or in the app.
  • Ask for a copy or a correction of your data by emailing us.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have rights to access, correct and erase your personal data and to nominate someone to act for you. We will respond to a verified request within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored on access-controlled servers. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone locked.

8. Children

Bhagawan AI is not for children under 13. If you are under 18 please use it with a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and we will tell you in the app if a change is significant.
